# Scores

See [methodology](methodology.md#evolutionary-trace-score-ets). Constraint and divergence are separate descriptive summaries computed from conservation/occupancy and normalized entropy/occupancy. They are correlated views of the same alignment evidence and must not be added as independent support. Missing domains, motifs, selection tests, or structure are represented as unavailable and never assigned zero evidence. ETS does not establish adaptation, function, or causality.
